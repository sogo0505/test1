Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Neil Harness is a private personal automation tool used only by its owner.

Data accessed

The application may access files it creates or manages in the owner's Google Drive, analytics and video metadata from the owner's YouTube account, and basic profile information, public video metadata, and video performance counters from the owner's TikTok account.

Use and storage

This data is used only to support the Neil Lives Here production workflow and private performance analysis. Analytics snapshots may be stored in the owner's private GitHub repository.

Sharing

The application does not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or intentionally share connected-account data with third parties.

Revocation and deletion

Access can be revoked through the connected platform's account settings. Stored analytics snapshots can be deleted by the owner from the private repository.
