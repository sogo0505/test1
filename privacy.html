Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Neil Harness is a private personal automation tool used only by its owner.

Neil Harness requests the Google Drive drive.file permission so it can create, upload, organize, and manage production files in the owner's Google Drive as part of the Neil Lives Here workflow.

The application does not sell personal data, does not use Google Drive data for advertising, and does not intentionally share Google Drive data with third parties. The application is not offered to the public.

Google Drive access is used only to provide the application's production-storage and review functionality.
